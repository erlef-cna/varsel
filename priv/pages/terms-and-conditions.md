## What this service is for

Reporting vulnerabilities in BEAM ecosystem packages, coordinating them with the
people who can fix them, and publishing the resulting CVE records.

What is in scope for this CNA, and what makes something a CVE, are set out in
[Scope](/scope) and [CVE Criteria](/cve-criteria). Reports outside that scope
will be redirected or declined.

## Using it responsibly

Report vulnerabilities you have found in good faith. Do not use this service to
submit reports you know to be false, to publish someone else's personal data, or
to pursue anything unlawful.

Vulnerability coordination depends on discretion. If you are given access to a
case, treat what you learn there as confidential until it is published.

Automated access is available through our [API](/api-access) at a reasonable
volume. Do not scrape, overload, or attempt to reach cases or reports that are
not yours.

## Nothing here publishes itself

This is not a social network or a publishing platform. **Nothing anyone writes
here becomes public on its own.** Reports, comments and case content are visible
only to the people working the case, and what we publish as an advisory is
written and reviewed by the CNA before it goes out.

Case discussion is deliberately append-only, so that the record of a
coordination stays intact for everyone working it. That means we do not edit or
withdraw what people wrote. If something written here breaks these terms or the
law we will remove it, and where it warrants it remove the account that posted
it, but this is an operator intervention rather than a routine feature.

If you think something here should not be here, or you disagree with a decision
we have taken about your content or your account, [tell us](/contact) and a
person will look at it.
